Movies

What I like

Stories that commit to their premise completely — worlds built as systems, with the mechanism visible: Interstellar, Inception, The Prestige. Mysticism and science fiction in the same frame — Doctor Strange, and the Hindu-cosmology thread that runs under a lot of what I love. Adult animation that takes its ideas seriously: Pantheon, Arcane, Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex, Scavengers Reign, A Scanner Darkly. And a deep Telugu cinema base. What doesn't work for me: atmosphere with no mechanism underneath it.

Directors I follow

Christopher Nolan — three of the films above are his, and the pattern is the point: big premises, engineered like machines, played completely straight. The animation I follow runs on the same rule, whoever makes it — a world with a working logic, committed to all the way down.
