gated-dispatch

Approval-gated LLM dispatch: a prompt goes through deterministic verification, then a human approval gate, then a cost ledger — one ticket per run, whatever the outcome.

  • Metered under STD-0. Tickets are computed from the provider API's own usage fields at published rates — never estimates. Envelopes hard-stop at 100%, work holds at 90%, an unpriced model stops before dispatch.
  • Proven live. One ticketed round-trip per lane — Anthropic (claude-haiku-5-5, 19 in / 5 out, $0.000004), Gemini (gemini-3.8-flash, 6 in / 88 out, $0.000334), xAI (grok-4.20 non-reasoning, 190 in / 2 out, $0.000243). Total proof spend: $0.000581.
  • Standard library only. Python ≥3.11, no dependencies; 12 offline tests green.

Design records: ADRs + diagrams — including why tickets come from usage fields, not estimates, and why the ledger stores prompt hashes, never raw prompts. Extracted from a private working system; more of that system surfaces here as it is cleared for release.
