Principles

The working rules the public work runs on — one line each:

  • Proof, not promises. A claim gets checked at the layer that acts, or it gets labeled as unverified.
  • Nothing spends without a check and an approval. Cost is a decision, made before the run, not a surprise after it.
  • Measure from the source. Never estimate what the system itself can count.
  • Every push is a public statement. Write, build, and ship like someone will read it — someone will.
  • Small verified steps over big reveals. A thing that is checked beats a thing that is announced.
