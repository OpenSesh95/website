What a receipt shows — and what it doesn't

Each receipt shows what shipped, when, the outcome, the counts and hashes that let anyone check it, and a pointer to the source. Deliberately withheld, always: account and project identifiers, email addresses, network addresses and hostnames, full case or request IDs, anything credential-adjacent, and employer specifics. Money appears at envelope level only, and only in figures already public. The withholding is part of the proof.
