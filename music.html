Music

Started on alt rock. The archive years added classic rock, hip-hop, 2010s pop and EDM, and lofi. Now: electronic first — with Bollywood underneath it, lifelong, and a devotional shelf underneath everything.

The archive itself — eight playlists, three and a half thousand tracks — lives on my SoundCloud. One account I follow sets the collecting standard: @cdr_only, for trance and progressive remembered properly — original-era versions over remixes.
